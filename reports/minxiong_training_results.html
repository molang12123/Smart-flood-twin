目前物理假設

  • DEM valid pixels are the active domain; NoData is excluded and elevation sign is unrestricted.
  • Dry initial condition.
  • All exposed mask faces are treated as no-flow walls pending surveyed boundary conditions.
  • Spatially uniform constant Manning n=0.03 pending a roughness raster.
  • Each observed event is represented by its mean intensity and contiguous positive-hour duration.
  • No drainage, infiltration, river stage, buildings, or pump operation is represented.
